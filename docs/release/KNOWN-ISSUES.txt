Prey2006 Reawakened - known issues
==================================

- Feeding Tower: blood sprayed onto the masher slabs can flicker as they move
  away from you.
- The portal gun can bypass scripted events and reach places the campaign did
  not expect. Save before experimenting.
- Saves made while the Doom 3 weapons or portal gun were in use need that
  content to load; add it again with "Import games..." if you removed it.
- Multiplayer has not been tested.
- Windows 10/11 x64 only. Linux (native or Proton) has not been tested.
